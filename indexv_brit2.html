Background

I'm an applied machine learning engineer based in Toronto, Canada. I specialize in end-to-end architectures that bridge modeling and infrastructure — whether that is parsing 77,000 aviation safety document chunks through dense vector retrieval (BGE-M3 in Qdrant) and Neo4j knowledge graphs, or wiring LightGBM forecasting directly into ERP reordering engines.

I hold a B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering from Carleton University and postgraduate credentials in Applied A.I. Solution Development from George Brown College, backed by Azure AI Engineer Associate and AWS Solutions Architect – Associate certifications.
